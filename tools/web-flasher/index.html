1 · Free the serial port

A running AgentDeck daemon holds /dev/cu.*, and every open resets the board. This is the most common cause of a failed flash — and this page cannot detect it (an HTTPS page may not probe your local daemon).

Verify with lsof /dev/cu.* — it should print nothing.
